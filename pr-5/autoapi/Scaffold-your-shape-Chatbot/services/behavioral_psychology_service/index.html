Behavioral Psychology Service for Pili Coach Agent.

This service applies behavioral psychology principles to improve user motivation, adherence, and long-term fitness success through: - Habit formation strategies - Motivation enhancement techniques - Behavioral change interventions - Personalized coaching approaches
