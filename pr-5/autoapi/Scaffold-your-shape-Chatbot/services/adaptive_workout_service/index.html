Scaffold-your-shape-Chatbot.services.adaptive_workout_service

Adaptive Workout Generation Service for Pili Coach Agent.

This service provides intelligent, personalized workout generation based on: - User fitness goals and preferences - Current fitness level and progress - Available time and equipment constraints - Historical performance data - Behavioral psychology principles
